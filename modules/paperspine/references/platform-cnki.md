# CNKI Observation Record (Legacy Compatibility)

This reference keeps older `detection_platform=cnki` configurations readable.
PaperSpine does not claim knowledge of CNKI's internal AIGC classifier and does
not optimize prose against an inferred threshold.

Follow `references/humanize.md`: use an authorized author corpus, revise only
after claims and evidence are frozen, prefer deletion of boilerplate over
synonym substitution, preserve the semantic invariants, and require independent
audit plus author confirmation. Clean author-written prose must remain a no-op.

An author-supplied CNKI report may be stored only as a hash-bound external
observation. It cannot override semantic audit results or become a readiness
gate. Never promise a pass or output a fabricated AI percentage. See
`references/humanize-calibration.md`.
