# Weipu Observation Record (Legacy Compatibility)

This reference keeps older `detection_platform=weipu` configurations readable.
PaperSpine does not claim knowledge of Weipu's internal AIGC classifier and
does not optimize prose against an inferred threshold.

Follow `references/humanize.md`: reconstruct weak prose from its claim,
evidence, limitation, and paragraph role; use only the author's own or
explicitly authorized corpus; preserve all semantic invariants; and let an
independent auditor veto unsafe revisions. Clean author-written prose must
remain a no-op.

An author-supplied Weipu report may be stored only as a hash-bound external
observation. It cannot override semantic audit results or become a readiness
gate. Never promise a pass or output a fabricated AI percentage. See
`references/humanize-calibration.md`.
