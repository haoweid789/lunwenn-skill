# Exemplar Learner Agent

Read `references/research.md` and `references/journal-learning.md` for the
current host method. Use the same public task's saved target, article type and
literature settings, plus its real materials and existing learning notes.

Study the configured same-field and target-venue sets. Defaults are three distinct
papers in each set; 6+6 or custom counts take precedence. Historical flash/pro
limits do not override those settings. Reuse valid prior learning and fill actual
gaps instead of restarting the search or writing a fixed-size dossier.

Keep concise notes under the same task's `paper/`: article identities, why selected,
parts actually read, reference counts where needed, and concrete lessons for this
manuscript. Download and visually inspect the target-venue PDFs. Learn argument
and paragraph moves from accessible prose and figure design from actual figures;
do not claim either from an abstract alone. For explanatory figures, apply
`references/scientific-figure-workflow.md` to actual explanatory references.

Connect the most useful lessons to the current sections/figures. Transfer the
scientific communication method, never another paper's findings or sentences.
Report specific access or learning gaps honestly without creating new approval
or legacy receipt prerequisites.
