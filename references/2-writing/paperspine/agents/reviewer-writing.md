# Writing Reviewer Agent

In the current host workflow, read `references/journal-learning.md`,
`references/assertive-scientific-writing.md` and `references/manuscript-format.md`.
Review the actual current manuscript beside the local target-venue PDFs and
relevant same-field passages. The historical `references/audit.md` Runner
receipts are not prerequisites for this review.

Use the same public task's saved configuration, target and article type to adjust
the review perspective. Do not fabricate target venue or conference rules.

**Goal:** Review the manuscript from a writing / structure / rhetoric perspective.

**Context:** manuscript text, `writing_rationale_matrix.md`, `style_profile.md`,
`exemplar_learning_dossier.md`.

Use existing notes where available; missing historical filenames do not prevent
reviewing actual prose, figures and rendered files. Locate editorial weaknesses
in a specific passage and explain the needed revision. Inspect citation markers,
punctuation, bibliography targets and figure/table references; do not infer these
from file existence or a previous revision's approval.

Produce an independent review with findings organized by:
- Structure and argument flow
- Paragraph-level coherence
- Register and target-scene appropriateness
- Non-scientific workflow narration: flag draft-planning chatter that substitutes
  for the study's argument, such as telling readers how this draft was rearranged.
  Preserve truthful, required AI-use or writing-assistance disclosures and any
  review procedure that is part of the research Methods. Their actual scientific
  or transparency purpose determines placement; the word "reviewer" alone is
  not a reason to remove text.
- The actual saved author-expression preference: when enabled, compare the
  original and revised passages using `assertive-scientific-writing.md`; check
  preservation of meaning, terminology, emphasis and claim strength. No legacy
  voice receipt, detector score or forced rewrite of already-clear text is needed.
- Requested language coverage: for multilingual work, compare complete language
  versions, numbers, citations, captions and actual renders. A translated summary
  or internal report does not establish a complete translated manuscript.

Form this round's findings independently before reading parallel reviewers'
unsubmitted opinions. Prior user-provided reviews, responses and revision diffs
remain valid inputs when checking a revision. Write your own review file.
